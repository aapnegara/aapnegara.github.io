Background

Hello! I'm Andi Agung Prawira Negara (Agung Negara), a Master's student in Astronomy and Astrophysics at KU Leuven. I work in theoretical astrophysics, and I am a computational person at heart: I learn by building models and running them. I have recently started learning plasma astrophysics, and I am especially interested in space weather and in using machine learning to study it.

Before KU Leuven, I earned my bachelor's degree in Physics at Universitas Hasanuddin. There I used the one-dimensional stellar evolution code MESA to study how stronger mass loss in the red supergiant phase changes the evolution of massive stars.

At KU Leuven, I study the circumstellar environments of asymptotic giant branch (AGB) stars: the slow winds of gas and dust these stars shed near the end of their lives. My thesis combines computational modelling, astrochemistry and radiative transfer to trace the physics and chemistry in these winds, and to link the models more closely to what telescopes observe.

Research interests

  • Stellar evolution
